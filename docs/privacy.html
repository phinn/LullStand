Privacy Policy

LullStand · Effective: 2026-10-28

Zero data collection

LullStand does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising, no tracking.

All video and audio stay on your local network. The camera stream goes directly from one of your devices to the other over your own Wi-Fi, peer-to-peer. It never touches any server.

Permissions we request

  • Camera — only when you use a device as the nursery station (the camera unit).
  • Microphone — cry detection and talk-back on the station device.
  • Local Network — to discover and connect the two devices on the same Wi-Fi.

Deny any permission and the app still works as the parent (viewing) unit.

What is stored on your device

Only your role choice (parent / station) and alert settings, kept in local preferences. Uninstalling removes everything.
